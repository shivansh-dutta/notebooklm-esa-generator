# All Appropriate Inquiries (AAI) — 40 CFR Part 312

> Source: 40 CFR Part 312, promulgated under CERCLA 42 U.S.C. § 9601(35)(B). This regulation establishes the standards and practices for conducting all appropriate inquiries to qualify for the innocent landowner, contiguous property owner, and bona fide prospective purchaser (BFPP) liability protections under CERCLA.

---

## Purpose (40 CFR §312.1)

The purpose of this part is to establish standards and practices for the conduct of all appropriate inquiries as required under sections 101(35)(B)(ii) and (iii), 107(q)(1)(A)(viii), and 107(r)(1) of the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA), 42 U.S.C. §§ 9601(35)(B)(ii) and (iii), 9607(q)(1)(A)(viii), and 9607(r)(1). Compliance with this part constitutes compliance with the all appropriate inquiries requirements of CERCLA for purposes of qualifying for the landowner liability protections (LLPs) provided under CERCLA §§ 101(35), 107(b)(3), 107(q), and 107(r).

---

## Key Requirements (40 CFR §312.20)

The following inquiries must be conducted and documented to satisfy AAI requirements:

1. **Interviews with past and present owners, operators, and occupants** — The environmental professional shall conduct interviews with the current owner and/or operator of the subject property, and with major occupants of the subject property, for the purpose of gathering information regarding the potential for contamination at the subject property.

2. **Review of historical sources** — The environmental professional shall review and evaluate information gathered from standard historical sources to identify *recognized environmental conditions* in connection with the subject property. Standard historical sources include aerial photographs, Sanborn fire insurance maps, city directories, and USGS topographic maps.

3. **Review of federal, state, tribal, and local government records** — The environmental professional shall review and evaluate federal, state, tribal, and local government records with respect to the subject property and properties within the approximate minimum search distances specified in ASTM E1527-21 Table 1.

4. **Visual inspection of the subject property and adjoining properties** — The environmental professional shall conduct a visual on-site inspection of the subject property and a visual inspection of the subject property from the nearest accessible public thoroughfare or adjacent properties.

5. **Declaration by environmental professional** — The environmental professional shall prepare and sign a written declaration regarding the performance of the all appropriate inquiries (see §312.21).

6. **Specialized knowledge or experience** — The environmental professional shall take into account specialized knowledge or experience of the user or environmental professional with respect to the type of facility or area where the subject property is located.

7. **Relationship of purchase price to fair market value** — The environmental professional shall take into account the relationship between the purchase price of the subject property and the fair market value of the subject property if the property were not contaminated.

8. **Commonly known or reasonably ascertainable information** — The environmental professional shall take into account commonly known or reasonably ascertainable information about the subject property.

9. **Degree of obviousness of contamination** — The environmental professional shall take into account the degree of obviousness of the presence or likely presence of contamination at the subject property.

## Standard Search Distances (ASTM E1527-21 Table 1)

| Database | Minimum Search Radius |
|---|---|
| NPL (National Priorities List / Superfund) | 1.0 mile |
| Delisted NPL | 0.5 mile |
| CERCLIS (SEMS) | 0.5 mile |
| CERCLIS NFRAP (SEMS-ARCHIVE) | 0.5 mile |
| RCRA CORRACTS TSD Facilities | 1.0 mile |
| RCRA non-CORRACTS TSD Facilities | 0.5 mile |
| RCRA Generators (LQG, SQG, CESQG) | Adjoining properties only |
| ERNS (Emergency Response Notification System) | Subject property only |
| State equivalent NPL / State Hazardous Waste Sites | 1.0 mile |
| State equivalent CERCLIS | 0.5 mile |
| State landfill and/or solid waste disposal sites | 0.5 mile |
| State leaking underground storage tank (LUST) lists | 0.5 mile |
| State registered UST lists | 0.25 mile |
| State voluntary cleanup sites | 0.5 mile |
| State Brownfields sites | 0.5 mile |
| Institutional controls / engineering controls registries | 0.5 mile |
| State spills/releases databases | 0.5 mile |

*Note: These are ASTM E1527-21 Table 1 minimum search distances. Actual distances used in any specific EDR report may exceed these minimums.*

## Environmental Professional Qualifications (40 CFR §312.10(b))

A person may qualify as an environmental professional for purposes of 40 CFR Part 312 if the person meets one of the following criteria:

1. **Licensed PE or PG** — Holds a current Professional Engineer (PE) or Professional Geologist (PG) license or registration from a state, tribe, or U.S. territory (or the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico) AND has the equivalent of three (3) years of full-time relevant experience in the performance of environmental site assessments.

2. **Licensed Environmental Professional** — Holds a current license or certification as an environmental professional from a state, tribe, or U.S. territory AND has the equivalent of three (3) years of full-time relevant experience in the performance of environmental site assessments.

3. **Degree in Engineering or Science** — Has a baccalaureate or higher degree from an accredited institution of higher education in a discipline of engineering or science AND has the equivalent of five (5) years of full-time relevant experience in the performance of environmental site assessments.

4. **Ten Years Experience** — Has the equivalent of ten (10) years of full-time relevant experience in the performance of environmental site assessments.

## EP Declaration Requirements (40 CFR §312.21)

The environmental professional must prepare, sign, and date a written declaration stating:

> "I declare that, to the best of my professional knowledge and belief, I meet the definition of Environmental Professional as defined in §312.10 of this part. I have the specific qualifications based on education, training, and experience to assess a property of the nature, history, and setting of the subject property. I have developed and performed all appropriate inquiries in conformance with the standards and practices set forth in 40 CFR Part 312."

---

## Timing Requirement (40 CFR §312.20(a))

- All components of AAI must be completed or updated within **180 days prior to** the date of acquisition of the subject property (or prior to the date of the intended transaction for non-acquisition transactions).
- Alternatively, all components may be completed within **one year prior to** the date of acquisition, provided that the following five components are updated within the 180-day period: (i) interviews; (ii) searches for recorded environmental cleanup liens; (iii) reviews of federal, state, tribal, and local government records; (iv) visual inspections; and (v) the EP declaration.
